About this app

no naked numbers · every figure traces to a checksummed regulator file

Wells, geometry and production rebuilt from public regulator files, with the resident dataset inventory and its scope reported live on the Status surface rather than claimed here. Where a regulator publishes only lease-level volumes, well-level production stays pending allocation rather than being inferred. The Status surface reports bounded infrastructure checks, scheduled work, exact-grain dataset inventory, and durable source-poll freshness. Each source shows its latest attempt and outcome, next expected poll, cadence, retrieval and declared vintages, and latest artifact identity. Unchanged checks can keep older bytes current; failed or interrupted checks cannot. Remote backup-copy evidence remains an explicit limit; weekly logical restore results are persisted and freshness-checked, and the nightly 90-day ephemeral-lineage retention job is observed alongside the other schedules. Viewport summaries that persist exact response lineage are capped at 30 requests per principal per UTC minute. Click any ⌾ derivation handle to trace a served figure to a checksummed file. Well cards now keep source-observed completion events separate from regulator pool-to-formation mappings; design measurements and formation tops remain explicitly unserved. Lateral-bearing North Dakota cards also show current physical neighbours with strict earlier-completion cutoffs and exact lineage; proximity is not presented as a model analog. Forecasts are not live: P3 readiness now captures append-only FracFocus completion anchors without a spud fallback. The resident load anchors 17,563 of 43,817 ND wells and leaves uncovered dates null; reviewed aliases cover every current ND pool label. Matrix version fv2.0 freezes the earliest source pool month, publishes missing and simultaneous-conflict coverage, and records the measured 82-day median source lag. The resident matrix and model-ready mdv1.4 dataset replay byte-identically with 105,378 three-stream labels, explicit censoring/rejection coverage, and eight shared rolling splits. Source-matched historical formation context is repaired without cross-manifest inference. Missing lateral context remains explicitly unavailable when the source has no reported lateral. The accepted 2026-08-28 publication preserves unchanged tcv1.0 and all eight split hashes; two complete builds reproduce every artifact byte-identically and an independent read rehashes them against the receipt. It passes the 5% ceiling at 230 of 21,300 TEST instances. The ladder is not widened; forecasts are still not served.
